Quiz 2 · Question 1

From knowing formulas to solving integrals.

Your job now is to recognize the structure, choose the right method, and show clean steps. Question 1 has two parts.

1(a) Indefinite integralFind the antiderivative and include +C.
1(b) Definite integralFind F(b) − F(a); no +C in the final answer.

First: choose the method

Before writing anything, look at the shape of the integrand.

Basic rule

Sums of powers, constants, exponentials, or other forms whose antiderivatives you know directly.

Substitution

You see an “inside function” and its derivative nearby. Think: let u equal the inside.

Integration by parts

You see a product such as x·ex, x·ln x, or a polynomial multiplied by another function.

Simplify first

Expand, split a fraction, cancel factors, or rewrite roots as powers before integrating.

The exam-safe method

Use this short routine for both parts of Question 1.

  1. Identify: indefinite or definite?
  2. Simplify: rewrite roots, fractions, or brackets if useful.
  3. Choose: basic rule, substitution, or integration by parts.
  4. Integrate: show enough working to make the method clear.
  5. Finish correctly: +C for indefinite; F(b) − F(a) for definite.
  6. Check: signs, powers, limits, and arithmetic.

Part A · Indefinite integral

The answer is a family of antiderivatives, so it must end with +C.

Worked example · substitution

A

Evaluate:

∫ 2x(x² + 3)4 dx
Let u = x² + 3, so du = 2x dx.
∫ u4 du = u5/5 + C
Answer: (x² + 3)5/5 + C
Most common lost mark: forgetting +C. Write it as soon as you finish the antiderivative.

Practice A · Indefinite

Solve on paper first. Reveal the answer only after completing your steps.

A1 · Basic
∫ (3x² − 4x + 5) dx
x³ − 2x² + 5x + C
A2 · Negative power
∫ (2/x³) dx
−1/x² + C
A3 · Substitution
∫ 6x(x² + 1)² dx
(x² + 1)³ + C
A4 · Log form
∫ 5/(5x − 2) dx
ln|5x − 2| + C
A5 · By parts
∫ x ex dx
ex(x − 1) + C
A6 · Simplify
∫ (x² + 2x)/x dx
x²/2 + 2x + C

Part B · Definite integral

Find an antiderivative, then substitute the bounds in the correct order.

∫ab f(x) dx = [F(x)]ab = F(b) − F(a)

Worked example · Fundamental Theorem

B

Evaluate:

∫02 (3x² + 1) dx
F(x) = x³ + x
[x³ + x]02 = (2³ + 2) − (0³ + 0)
Answer: 10
Remember: upper minus lower, keep parentheses around each substituted expression, and do not write +C in the final answer.

Practice B · Definite

Show the antiderivative and the bound substitution—not only the number.

B1 · Basic
∫02 3x² dx
[x³]02 = 8
B2 · Polynomial
∫13 (2x + 1) dx
[x² + x]13 = 10
B3 · Substitution
∫01 2x(x² + 1)³ dx
[(x² + 1)4/4]01 = 15/4
B4 · Negative result
∫02 (x − 2) dx
[x²/2 − 2x]02 = −2
B5 · Log form
∫1e 1/x dx
[ln x]1e = 1
B6 · Reversed bounds
∫20 x dx
[x²/2]20 = −2
